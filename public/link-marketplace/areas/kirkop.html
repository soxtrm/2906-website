From St Leonard’s square

The church towers give Kirkop a landmark. The prehistoric menhir, local sports grounds and bus stops tell you more about how the village fits into this part of Malta.

Two landmarks, different histories

The parish stands on Misraħ San’ Anard. The menhir takes Kirkop’s history much further back, to a period whose traces are harder to interpret.

St Leonard’s parish

Start in the square for a view of the dome, the twin bell towers and the balustrade. The church is recorded as item 01870 in the national heritage inventory. Check with the parish before planning an interior visit.

Archdiocese parish information ↗

The menhir

The local council identifies the standing stone as a prehistoric monolith and a symbol of Kirkop. Its original purpose remains uncertain.
