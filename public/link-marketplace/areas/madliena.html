WHY THE STREET MATTERS

Madliena changes with the contour.

Swieqi Local Council describes Wied il-Kbir, also called Wied Mejxu, running from Għargħur through Madliena towards St Andrew's Road. That supports a terrain-led viewing checklist, but it does not establish a safe continuous footpath, a legal district boundary or the gradient at a particular home.

Use the address pin to test the journey. Keep a locality label as context, not as a substitute for the route.
