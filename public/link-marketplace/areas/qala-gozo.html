READ THE LOCALITY IN PARTS

A Gozo base with more than one context.

ARRIVING AND MOVING AROUND

WHAT TO CHECK AT A VIEWING

The street matters more than the label.

Confirm gradient, pavement continuity, evening noise, parking arrangement, entrance access, nearest useful stop and the actual route to daily errands. A Qala label or locality centroid cannot answer those questions.
