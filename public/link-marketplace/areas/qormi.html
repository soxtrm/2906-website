From San Ġorġ to San Sebastjan

The Archdiocese records San Ġorġ as a parish by 1436. The present church dates to 1584, and its dome and towers came later. The church on Triq il-Kbira is a useful starting point for the older streets around it.

San Sebastjan became a separate parish in 1936. Its history distinguishes the older church by the statue from the larger parish church built nearby. Check the relevant parish listing for service times and visiting arrangements.

Bread and everyday life

Casal Fornaro

The San Sebastjan parish archive documents a community bread festival. UNESCO’s safeguarding record for Maltese ftira also describes the work of Casal Fornaro organisers in Qormi. Maypole identifies its production facility in Tal-Ħandaq and lists retail locations separately.
