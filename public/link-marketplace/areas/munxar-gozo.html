Village life around the parish

St Paul’s church provides a starting point for the inland village. Visit Gozo dates its construction to 1914–1925 and the creation of the independent parish to December 1957. In its 2026 feature, the tourism authority also identifies the St Paul statue as a 1933 work by Wistin Camilleri.

Everyday supplies

MySupermarket Munxar’s merchant page lists the shop on Triq Profs Guze Aquilina and shows grocery categories. Check the current stock and service arrangements for your visit. This inland shop is separate from the supermarket at Xlendi Bay.

Council services

The council office is on Profs. Ġuże’ Aquilina Street. Its services directory links to waste management, maintenance and free internet access information. Follow the relevant service page for current arrangements rather than relying on an old collection schedule.

A meal at Xlendi

For waterfront dining, Ta’ Karolina publishes its menu and booking information. The restaurant is at Xlendi Bay, a separate destination from Munxar’s parish square. Plan the meal around your onward journey and confirm a table with the restaurant.

From the square to the coast

The tourism authority’s Munxar feature places the inland village between valleys, with Sanap and Ras il-Bajjada on its coastal edge and Xlendi below the village core. The photograph shows Sanap’s cliffs in 2017, rather than the parish square.

A coastal outing needs its own approach and weather check. The church marker below is an orientation point in the village; it supplies no path, distance or walking time to the cliffs. Choose a verified land route and check current access before setting off.

Back in the village, Visit Gozo records wartime shelters beneath the square, constructed in 1942. Ask the council about any current visiting arrangements. Their historical existence does not establish that they are open to visitors today.

Plan around the right stop

Route 306 lists Munxar stop 4007 and Xlendi stop 1827. Choose the village or the bay as your destination, then check direction and date in the journey planner. A stop listed for the wider area does not establish a direct connection to a particular accommodation.

The separate Victoria guide and Fontana guide can help with a wider shopping or services trip. Keep those destinations distinct when comparing an address, selecting a stop or arranging a pickup.

For village events, check a dated programme from the parish or council. General tourism descriptions place the festa in May but differ on the exact weekend. Use a current announcement before planning a visit around it.
