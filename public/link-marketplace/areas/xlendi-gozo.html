Southwestern Gozo

Ix-Xlendi

A bay at the foot of the cliffs. Come for the waterfront, plan a meal by the shore, or arrange a dive with a local operator. Xlendi has its own coastal character within the Munxar council area.

Choose your kind of bay day

A small beach, waterfront buildings and the surrounding cliffs frame Xlendi. Visit Gozo describes its origins as a fishing village. The bay is a destination in its own right; the inland village of Munxar and the coastal tower each deserve a separate place in your plans.

A waterfront meal

Ta’ Karolina locates itself at the right end of the waterfront, near the steps toward Karolina Cave. Its menu includes pasta, seafood, meat dishes and pizza. Browse the current menu and ask the restaurant about a table for your date.

Arrange a dive

Gozo Dive St Andrew’s is based at Xlendi Bay. Its website describes beginner, guided and technical diving options. Discuss your experience and the conditions with the operator before choosing an activity; the programme depends on the dive you can safely undertake.

Bay and village

Xlendi falls within Munxar Local Council. The council’s office is inland on Profs. Ġuże’ Aquilina Street in Munxar. Use its notices and service pages for local matters, and keep the office’s location distinct from the waterfront.

Look beyond the waterfront

Visit Gozo’s Xlendi guide identifies the coastal watchtower, built in 1650, as a landmark separate from the beach. Choose the tower as its own destination and check the current approach before setting off.

The cliffs and coastal paths are part of Xlendi’s wider setting. The official Xlendi walking leaflet gives historical planning context. Current weather and access still need checking locally, particularly around the steps and exposed shoreline.

The map below marks a bus platform beside the bay. It draws no walking line across the water or toward the tower. Use a verified land route for a coastal outing, and check sea conditions before entering the water.

Plan the rest of the day

Route 306 and route 330 connect Victoria and Xlendi. Both operator pages identify Xlendi stop 1827. Route 306 also lists Munxar stop 4007. Select your direction and date in the journey planner before travelling.

A wider shopping or services trip can be planned with the separate Victoria guide and Fontana guide. Those inland destinations have their own context. Check the actual business and its current service arrangements before making a special journey.

For an Xlendi stay, compare the accommodation’s actual street and access with the activities you intend to do. A photograph of the bay does not establish a particular home’s view or an accessible route from its door. Use the precise address when checking transport and arrival arrangements.
