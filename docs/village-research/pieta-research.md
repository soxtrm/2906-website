## Editorial boundary

Pietà must not be written as one flat waterfront district. The Local Council explicitly describes two distinct settings that now form one district:

- lower Pietà at the inner part of Marsamxett Harbour, historically developed around a valley and a changed shoreline;
- higher Guardamangia on the promontory, around sixty metres above sea level in the Council account, with later urban development tied to the former St Luke's Hospital area;
- the marina/shore roads, inland residential streets and sloping connections between them, which require the exact address rather than a generic waterfront label;
- neighbouring Msida, Floriana, Valletta, Santa Venera and Ta' Xbiex, which remain separate localities even when one map viewport or marketing description overlaps them.

These are editorial reading aids, not administrative polygons. Do not infer a harbour view, level access, short walk, quiet street or proximity from the locality name.

Source: https://pietalc.gov.mt/en/homepage/geography-and-history/

## Verified locality and heritage context

### Pietà and Guardamangia

The Local Council says lower Pietà developed at the end of the former Wied il-Qasab valley at the inner Marsamxett Harbour, while Guardamangia developed on the higher promontory. It also documents the later role of St Luke's Hospital in joining the two urban areas. The edition may use this to explain why elevation and street character vary, but it must not convert the Council's history into a property-specific slope, view, traffic or access claim.

Source: https://pietalc.gov.mt/en/homepage/geography-and-history/

### Villa Guardamangia

Heritage Malta identifies Villa Guardamangia at Telgħa ta' Gwardamanġa, Pietà and describes it as an 18th-century villa that served as Princess Elizabeth's residence from 1949 to 1951. The project page also says major structural works are required. Treat it as a conservation project and orientation anchor; do not describe it as routinely open or promise visitor access.

Source: https://heritagemalta.mt/projects/villa-guardamangia/

### Villa Frere

Heritage Malta's event material documents the surviving Villa Frere gardens in Pietà and the work of Friends of Villa Frere. Event dates and special openings are historical evidence only. Any present access, programme or ticket claim needs a fresh operator check and should not be inferred from an old event page.

Source: https://heritagemalta.mt/news/discover-pietas-secret-garden/

## Activity and public-space treatment

- The marina and Marsamxett Harbour can orient the lower district, but a dated waterfront photograph is not evidence of today's berths, traffic, construction, view or access from a listing.
- Villa Guardamangia can anchor the higher district as a Heritage Malta conservation project, with no routine-opening claim.
- Villa Frere can appear only with a current access caveat unless a live operator programme is verified at release time.
- The future map must not draw a straight line, assumed walking route, water crossing, ferry or administrative boundary.
- Walking geometry, bus schedules, current car-traffic estimates and taxi-price observations remain separate sources with their own endpoints and timestamps.
