## Editorial structure and boundaries

Santa Venera Local Council places the locality between Birkirkara, Ħamrun and Qormi. The edition should preserve three distinct contexts:

1. the established residential and local-service streets around Triq il-Kbira San Ġużepp and the parish areas;
2. the Wignacourt Aqueduct, Casa Leoni and Romeo Romano heritage-and-garden group;
3. the busier central road edges and neighbouring-town interfaces, which must not be generalised to every Santa Venera address.

Birkirkara, Ħamrun and Qormi remain separate property-search and municipal contexts. A broad `Santa Venera` label must never imply a particular outlook, quietness, parking, walkability, road condition or distance to a named place. The exact listing address comes first.
