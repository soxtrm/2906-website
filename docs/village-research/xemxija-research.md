## Editorial boundary and naming

Use **Ix-Xemxija** at first mention and **Xemxija** thereafter. Current Malta Public Transport pages use `Ix-Xemxija`, while the public inventory and several operators use `Xemxija`. The Address Registrar provides the official locality lookup, and government/environment sources place Xemxija within the limits of San Pawl il-Baħar. The edition must not present it as a separate local council.

Keep San Pawl il-Baħar town, Buġibba, Qawra, Burmarrad, Wardija, Pwales, Mistra and Mellieħa editorially distinct. ## Bay, hillside and protected wetland

The edition should describe Xemxija as a built coastal-and-hillside settlement beside Xemxija Bay, with the Pwales landscape and Is-Simar as separate adjacent contexts. It must not flatten waterfront apartments, steep internal streets, the heritage ridge and wetland edge into one uniform lifestyle promise.

ERA identifies Is-Simar, within the limits of San Pawl il-Baħar, as a Ramsar wetland as well as part of the Natura 2000 network. BirdLife Malta, which manages the reserve, describes open pools, reedbeds and trees and publishes live access information. This supports a carefully bounded nature section. Seasonal opening days, hours, parking and access conditions must be linked live and rechecked instead of copied into evergreen prose.

## Heritage and walking context

ERA planning documentation records the Xemxija Area of Archaeological Importance on Bajda Ridge and lists multi-period features including tombs, cart ruts, an ancient road, quarries and old beehives. This supports a carefully bounded heritage-ridge section, but not an improvised route from a property.

Use only the published walk/heritage material and the existing route on the ground. Do not draw a straight line, call the trail step-free, guarantee maintenance or infer a walking time. Any future page should distinguish the ridge trail from ordinary pavement and the bay promenade, and it should advise readers to verify current access and terrain conditions.

Source:
