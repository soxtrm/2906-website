## Editorial boundary

Birżebbuġa must not be reduced to a single beach or a generic south-coast label. The edition should distinguish at least:

- the Pretty Bay and central waterfront context;
- the Qajjenza / St George's Bay side and Ferretti context;
- the inland valleys and heritage sites around Għar Dalam and Borġ in-Nadur;
- the Kalafrana, Bengħajsa and Freeport-facing edges, where the exact street and current conditions need checking rather than a locality-wide promise about views, noise, air, access or traffic;
- neighbouring Marsaxlokk and other south-eastern localities, which remain separate property contexts even when they share services or appear in one map viewport.

These are editorial reading aids, not administrative polygons. The future map should remain a broad click-to-load 2D orientation until an authoritative boundary source is integrated.

## Verified locality and heritage context

### Birżebbuġa Local Council

The council describes Birżebbuġa as a south-eastern seaside town between Kalafrana and Marsaxlokk and as the meeting point of Wied Dalam, Wied Żembaq, Wied Qoton and Wied Buni. Its geography-and-history page also places Għar Dalam and the Borġ in-Nadur remains within the wider locality context.

Safe use: establish the coastal and valley setting and explain why the exact part of the locality matters. Do not repeat the council page's unsourced walking-time wording, convert historical material into a current access promise, or suggest that every property is beside the beach or a heritage site.

Sources:

- https://birzebbugalc.gov.mt/en/homepage/geography-and-history/
- https://birzebbugalc.gov.mt/en/about-locality/

### Għar Dalam and Borġ in-Nadur

Heritage Malta identifies Għar Dalam Cave and Museum in Birżebbuġa and describes Borġ in-Nadur as a prehistoric complex on high ground between Wied Żembaq and Wied Dalam, facing the harbour entrance. Current opening hours, tickets and access arrangements must remain direct checks on the operator pages.

On 14 October 2026, the Borġ in-Nadur operator page carried an until-further-notice closure notice. The public edition records that review date and still directs readers to check the operator because the notice can change.

Sources:

- https://heritagemalta.mt/mt/explore/ghar-dalam/
- https://heritagemalta.mt/explore/borg-in-nadur/

### Address verification

The Office of the Address Registrar remains the correct current source for checking the official locality and street attached to a particular property. This is especially important around Qajjenza, Bengħajsa, Kalafrana and the Marsaxlokk-facing side.

Source: https://address.gov.mt/locate-a-street-search/

Primary HealthCare's current health-centres page places Birżebbuġa in the Paola Health Centre catchment. Its May 2026 Quality Service Charter also lists a B'Bugia community clinic at 148, B'Bugia Road. Services, referral arrangements and opening times can change and should be checked directly.

Sources:

- https://primaryhealthcare.gov.mt/en/health-centers/
- https://primaryhealthcare.gov.mt/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/May-2026-Quality-Service-Charter.pdf

## Food and social examples

### Ferretti

The operator gives its address as Triq il-Qajjenza, Birżebbuġa and describes a Mediterranean restaurant within historic walls. This can illustrate the Qajjenza side of the locality, but it is not a ranking, price guarantee, accessibility promise or claim about every nearby home.

Source: https://ferretti.com.mt/

If used, present them as licensed-location examples only. Menus, ratings, prices and hours require direct operator confirmation.
