Seven originals matched their Wikimedia Commons SHA-1 values. The selected outputs decoded at their stated dimensions and were inspected for framing and identifiable people, readable vehicle plates or private contact details. None were visible in the selected files. The earlier parish candidates 05 and 07 are interiors. The 01 and 02 exteriors show parked cars more prominently. These alternatives remain private. The photographed St Matthew building is the larger recorded church, and the temple image depicts Ħaġar Qim.
