## Editorial boundary

The Local Council describes historic Żejtun as two communities: the Lower Village, or *Ir-Raħal t'Isfel* / Bisqallin, and the Upper Village, or *Ir-Raħal ta' Fuq* / Ħal Ġwann / Ħal Bisbut. Its history page says the newer parish church was built between them. A future edition can use this as historical structure, while making clear that those names do not prove the exact modern position, frontage or walking access of any property.

Keep the central residential and parish context distinct from Bulebel and from the Tal-Barrani retail edge. A store may use a Żejtun search label while its operator page lists Għaxaq; that must not be rewritten as a village-core address. Żabbar, Fgura, Għaxaq, Marsaskala and Marsaxlokk remain separate localities. Every proximity claim needs a public address check, and every route needs real routing geometry.
