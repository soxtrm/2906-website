## Heritage and place character

The Government Restoration Directorate records that Grand Master Emmanuel de Rohan-Polduc elevated Żebbuġ to Città Rohan in 1777. Its account says the surviving De Rohan Arch was inaugurated in 1798, stands at the entrance to the main thoroughfare centred on Triq il-Kbira, entered the Antiquities List in 1935 and was designated Grade 1 in 2009. Those facts support a focused heritage section about the arch and the historic approach; they do not prove public interior access or a connected pedestrian itinerary.

VisitMalta lists a **Ħaż-Żebbuġ Heritage Trail** among its official walks. A later public edition may link to the live VisitMalta material as an optional self-guided activity. Route geometry, surface condition, duration and accessibility must be checked from the current trail document before any detailed claim is published; no straight line or improvised walking route should be drawn.

The Archdiocese of Malta lists the 2026 St Philip of Agira feast for Ħaż-Żebbuġ. This can support a carefully dated culture note, but the date must be rechecked for the publication year rather than written as a permanent annual schedule.
