## Name, administration and editorial boundary

Use **Paola** in the English title. Use **Raħal Ġdid** where a Maltese public source uses that name. Malta's Address Registrar lists Paola in the Port Region; this dossier does not manufacture a single hybrid official name.

Paola Local Council records the foundation of the planned town by Grand Master Fra Antoine de Paule on 20 July 1626, its original name “Paula”, a grid street plan and the early church of St Ubaldesca. Those facts support a concise civic-history section. They do not establish that a current listing sits inside the original grid, beside a church or on a historic street.

Fgura, Tarxien, Marsa and the Cottonera localities must remain separate. Paola's civic grid, the Ħal Saflieni area and the Kordin or Corradino context must also not be collapsed into one generic streetscape. The locality centroid proves nothing about a listing's exact neighbourhood, view, road width, parking, pavement continuity or walking time.

## Heritage that genuinely belongs to Paola

Heritage Malta places the Ħal Saflieni Hypogeum in Raħal Ġdid (Paola). Its current site page describes an underground burial complex cut into limestone, arranged over three levels and used between 4000 BC and 1500 BC. It was discovered during building work in 1902 and inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 1981. This is a Paola landmark, but it is capacity-controlled heritage with live booking and access conditions. A public edition should link to the current operator page rather than freeze ticket prices, opening hours or availability.

Heritage Malta also places Kordin III in Raħal Ġdid (Paola), near the Corradino Industrial Estate. Its site page explains that Kordin III is the surviving Kordin complex and documents the paved forecourt and multiple quern. The current page advertises limited regular openings. Access remains volatile and must be rechecked; the brochure must not turn a heritage record into an everyday-open promise.

Tarxien Prehistoric Complex remains in Tarxien. The Three Cities remain their own localities. They may appear as wider-area context only after their actual location, access and route have been verified.

Sources:

- [Heritage Malta — Ħal Saflieni Hypogeum](https://heritagemalta.mt/mt/explore/hal-saflieni-hypogeum/)
- [Heritage Malta — Kordin III Megalithic Site](https://heritagemalta.mt/explore/kordin-iii/)
