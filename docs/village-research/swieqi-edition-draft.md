A listing that says Swieqi is only the beginning. Compare Swieqi proper, Tal-Ibrag and Madliena separately, then check the home's actual street. Pembroke is a neighbouring locality with its own council and deserves its own search.

## Start with your weekly shop

Greens lists its Swieqi supermarket on Uqija Street in Ibrag. Its groceries, fresh-food counters and household range make it a useful destination to test for the weekly shop. Delivery and collection offer alternatives to carrying everything home. Add the store to your property shortlist and check the journey from each home; an area-wide distance cannot tell you whether the return walk with shopping will suit you.

## Three names, three separate searches

**Swieqi proper:** use this as a specific area choice alongside the broader municipal search. Confirm the listing pin before comparing daily journeys.

**Tal-Ibrag:** put the supermarket and your recurring destinations on the same map. Check the actual pavement, road crossings and entrance when viewing a home.

**Madliena:** explore the local heritage context, including the fort, chapel and Victoria Lines listed by Swieqi Council. A landmark pin does not guarantee public entry or a walkable connection; check access before planning an outing.

## Make the viewing useful

Try the journey you will repeat most often. Check it in the mode you expect to use, and look at the return trip too. At the property, check pavement continuity, steps, lift access and where deliveries can stop. These are viewing prompts, not claims that every home has the same conditions.
