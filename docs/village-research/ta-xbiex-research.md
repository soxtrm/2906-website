## Editorial boundary

The national Address Registrar lists Ta’ Xbiex as its own local council in Malta’s Eastern Region. The Local Council describes a peninsula whose seafront curves around a hill overlooking Marsamxett Harbour, and explains the name as deriving from *tax-xbiek*, “place of nets”. Those council statements support a waterfront-and-inland editorial structure; they do not prove a harbour view, quiet street, marina access or walking distance for any listing.

Keep Ta’ Xbiex distinct from neighbouring Gżira, Msida and Pietà. In particular, “Ta’ Xbiex seafront” can appear in addresses outside the Ta’ Xbiex council locality, so each venue or service must follow its operator or official address rather than the road name alone. The marina edge, the residential streets uphill and the professional-service addresses around the peninsula should not be flattened into one lifestyle promise.
