# Attard village edition research

Status: PUBLISHED — live verified 16 October 2026

Checked: 16 October 2026

## Editorial boundary

Attard must not be presented as one compact, uniformly walkable village. The
Local Council describes a locality of roughly seven square kilometres with a
historic village core, the Sant'Anton quarter, Ta' Qali to the north,
Ta' l-Idward and Misrah Kola, Tax-Xarolla / Taht ir-Rahal, Ta' Fgieni and
remaining agricultural or rural land. A future edition should therefore ask
for the exact street before making any claim about access to the core, San
Anton Gardens, Ta' Qali or neighbouring Balzan and Lija.

The page should use three practical contexts rather than an invented district
boundary:

1. the parish-centred older core;
2. Sant'Anton and the garden / Villa Bologna side;
3. outer residential streets and the much wider Ta' Qali / rural edge.

No generic walking time, commute, quietness, parking or garden proximity may
be inferred from an `Attard` locality label.

## Verified sources

### Locality and neighbourhood structure

- Attard Local Council, English homepage:
  https://hattardlc.gov.mt/en/
  - Describes the central-Malta setting, approximate municipal area and the
    named sub-areas used for the editorial boundary above.
  - Distinguishes residential, agricultural, rural, recreational and national
    sports / market functions within the wider locality.
- Attard Local Council, Geography and History:
  https://hattardlc.gov.mt/en/homepage/geography-and-history/
  - Supports the Three Villages relationship with Balzan and Lija and the
    long-standing garden / citrus context.
- Attard Local Council, Churches and Chapels:
  https://hattardlc.gov.mt/en/churches-chapels/
  - Supports the independent-parish history and the seventeenth-century
    parish-church context. It does not prove current visitor access.

### Health and everyday services

- Primary HealthCare, Rabat Health Centre:
  https://primaryhealthcare.gov.mt/en/health-centers/rabat-health-center/
  - Lists Attard in the Rabat Health Centre catchment and lists Attard among
    its peripheral community clinics.
  - Timetables and individual services are current operational information;
    a public edition should link users to the source for a fresh check rather
    than freeze detailed hours in evergreen copy.
- MyConvenience / MySupermarket locations:
  https://my.mt/locations/
  - Lists MySupermarket Attard at Triq l-Ghenba. This is an operator source for
    one practical shopping anchor, not evidence that every listing is nearby.
- The Food Coop:
  https://www.foodcoop.mt/
  - Operator site places the community-owned food shop at Triq il-Pitkali,
    Attard. Treat it as an example rather than a locality-wide recommendation.

### Food and activity examples

- The Villa Bologna Restaurant:
  https://www.thevillabolognarestaurant.com/
  - Operator site identifies a family-run Italian restaurant and bar at
    30 Triq San Anton, within the Villa Bologna gardens.
  - Do not repeat its hours without a release-day recheck and do not rank it.
- Government of Malta press release, 1 August 2026:
  https://www.gov.mt/en/Government/DOI/Press%20Releases/Pages/2026/08/01/pr261291en.aspx
  - Confirms phased conservation works at San Anton Gardens. The release says
    unaffected sections can remain open while works move between phases.
  - A future edition must flag works and require a current access check; it
    must not promise that every path or section is open.

## Map and route rules

- Use a fast click-to-load OpenStreetMap overview centred broadly enough to
  show the village core, Sant'Anton and Ta' Qali context.
- Do not draw a municipal boundary unless it comes from an authoritative
  boundary dataset and is clearly labelled.
- Do not draw a straight line, walking route, road ETA or public-transport
  itinerary. Those are separate data products and require their own source and
  timestamp.
- Attard has no evidenced ferry segment in this dossier. No ferry marker or
  water route belongs on this page.
