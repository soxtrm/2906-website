## Place boundary

Mellieħa Local Council says that the locality of Mellieħa includes the nearby village of Manikata. A Manikata edition should therefore present a named village inside the wider Mellieħa council territory, not a second council or a synonym for Mellieħa town.

The existing Mellieħa edition cannot simply be copied. Mellieħa's hill town, Għadira/Mellieħa Bay, Selmun, Marfa and Ċirkewwa remain separate search contexts. Għajn Tuffieħa, Golden Bay and the Majjistral coast may be relevant regional orientation points, but their beaches, paths, parking and access conditions must not be attributed to every Manikata address. No official source reviewed supplies a convenient street-by-street property-search boundary, so every listing still needs its exact locality, street, entrance and frontage checked.

Primary source: https://melliehalc.gov.mt/en/

## Three contexts to keep distinct

1. **Village and parish context.** The present parish church and the old St Joseph church are specific Manikata anchors. They do not prove that a listing overlooks a church, sits in the centre or has quiet access.
2. **Agricultural and cooperative context.** Koperattiva Rurali Manikata describes its work with local farmers and the restoration of Ir-Razzett tal-Qasam. This supports a real rural and agricultural setting, while remaining too narrow to describe every field, property, business or road.
3. **Outer roads and coast-facing approaches.** Manikata sits within a wider north-west landscape, but village roads, farmland, Għajn Tuffieħa, Golden Bay and the Majjistral coast are not interchangeable endpoints. A property page must check the exact route and terrain rather than infer a walk, beach access or view from a broad map.

## Everyday life and public services

### Health catchment

Primary HealthCare's May 2026 Quality Service Charter places Manikata in the Mosta Health Centre catchment and lists Mellieħa as the associated community clinic. This establishes the catchment only. It does not place a clinic in Manikata, identify the right service for a patient or promise appointments, opening hours or a journey time.

Source: https://primaryhealthcare.gov.mt/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/May-2026-Quality-Service-Charter.pdf

### Parish and heritage

Mellieħa Local Council lists Manikata Church among its churches and chapels. The Restoration Directorate separately records work at the old Manikata church, built in 1919/20 for the farming community and superseded in 1975 by a larger church. These sources support two distinct church contexts; they do not establish current public access, service times, parking, accessibility or a route through church property.

Sources:

- https://melliehalc.gov.mt/en/churches-chapels/
- https://restawr.gov.mt/en/projects/churches/
- https://www.gov.mt/en/Government/DOI/Press%20Releases/Pages/2019/July/05/pr191504en.aspx

### Rural cooperative and activities

Koperattiva Rurali Manikata says it was established in 2008 to support local farmers and safeguard rural life, and identifies Ir-Razzett tal-Qasam as the focus of its protection and restoration activity. Its published 2026 Summer Club ran from 30 June to 10 September and included supervised outdoor and educational activities. The programme is a dated operator record, not a permanent schedule or proof that a session is currently bookable. Any guide must link to the operator and require a current check for programme dates, prices, age rules, access and availability.

Sources:

- https://talqasam.mt/about-us/
- https://talqasam.mt/summer-school/
- https://tourism.gov.mt/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/5798-MOT-Eden-Awards-Brochure-V6_GC.pdf

### Food example

The Malta Tourism Authority's live restaurant register lists Country Bar and Restaurant under licence reference `RST-13232` at Triq il-Manikata, Mellieħa. It can serve as one regulator-backed local example. It is not a ranking, recommendation or complete dining directory, and the register does not justify publishing permanent hours, menu, prices, booking status, accessibility, parking or proximity to a property.

Source: https://mta.com.mt/licences/licences/?dir=desc&page=3&size=50&sort=EstablishmentName&type=restaurants

## Map and routing guardrails

Use a click-to-load OpenStreetMap orientation frame that shows the village/parish context, the agricultural edge and Ir-Razzett tal-Qasam without drawing an administrative or property-search boundary. District and venue pins should remain omitted until their coordinates and labels are independently verified.

No straight line may be presented as walking geometry. Walking, bus, current road traffic and taxi-price observations require separate sources, exact endpoints, mode and timestamp. No ferry is assumed for an ordinary Manikata property journey. A beach or coastal landmark must remain an orientation point unless a real route and current access information have been checked; no line across fields, protected land, cliffs or water becomes a journey.
