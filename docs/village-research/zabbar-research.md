## Editorial boundary and naming

Use **Ħaż-Żabbar** at first mention and **Żabbar** thereafter. The Local Council uses Ħaż-Żabbar and states that it belongs to the Port Region. Its current locality page also records the Hompesch city title and the historic approach through Fgura. These facts support naming and a bounded historic frame; the council's promotional descriptions of peace, countryside or coast must not be converted into a promise about a current home.

Keep Żabbar distinct from Fgura, Xgħajra, Kalkara, Marsaskala, Żejtun and the generic Cottonera/Three Cities label. The built core around the sanctuary and Hompesch Gate, newer residential edges, and roads towards neighbouring localities are not interchangeable. Xgħajra has its own local council and coastline; Kalkara has its own creek and harbour settings. A Żabbar locality label does not establish that a home is near either waterfront.

The single current ARGUS record discloses only the shared locality centroid **35.8756, 14.5361**. It does not establish a street, building, sanctuary proximity, historic-core position, view, parking arrangement, gradient, nearest stop or access route. Do not reuse listing language as an area-wide claim.

## Centre, sanctuary and historic context

The Local Council's current locality page records the 1797 Hompesch city title, Hompesch Gate on the approach from Paola through Fgura, and the older centre's connection to the sanctuary. The Archdiocese's current parish page identifies the Sanctuary of Our Lady of Graces, dates the present building's construction from 1641 to about 1696 and lists pastoral sites across the parish. This supports a clear centre-and-sanctuary narrative without implying that every listing is in the old core or near a church.

The Żabbar Sanctuary Museum appears in current VisitMalta search material and has an extensive Commons documentation category. No reliable operator page with current visitor access has yet cleared the publication standard. A public edition may mention the museum only as a local heritage institution and should tell readers to verify access through a current official channel; it must not freeze opening hours or imply guaranteed entry.

## Everyday services and transport

Route 94 is the separate Valletta–Xgħajra corridor and also passes named Żabbar stops. These pages establish current scheduled bus corridors, not a fixed journey time, frequency promise or nearest stop for a particular address. Keep bus timetables separate from walking, driving, ferry and taxi information.

Primary HealthCare's current Community Clinics page identifies Żabbar Community Clinic at the Civic Centre on Triq iċ-Ċawsli and explains that clinic services run on schedules and appointments. Its March 2026 service charter places Żabbar within a health-centre catchment. A public guide should link the live source for today's service and avoid promising availability or a property-specific route.

Scotts' current store locator identifies a Żabbar supermarket near Hompesch Gate. The Convenience Shop's own location page identifies two Żabbar branches. These are useful operator-verified everyday examples; they are not rankings, proof of what is nearest, or a parking/access guarantee. Opening hours and services remain on the live operator pages.
