## Editorial boundary and naming

Floriana is its own locality immediately outside Valletta, not a synonym for Valletta. The Floriana Local Council places the town between City Gate and Porte des Bombes and traces its fortifications to Pietro Paolo Floriani's seventeenth-century recommendations. The page may explain this gateway position, but it must not turn every address into a Valletta-centre, harbour-view or fortification-edge claim.

The public edition should keep at least four physical contexts distinct:

1. the lived-in civic streets and St Anne Street spine;
2. Pjazza San Publju / the Granaries and the parish-church setting;
3. the gardens, bastions and institutional edge around Argotti and the Msida Bastion Historic Garden;
4. the lower Xatt Pinto / waterfront context.

Valletta, Pietà, Marsa, Ħamrun and the waterfront/cruise-terminal context remain separately named places or edges even when a route or one map viewport includes more than one. A Floriana label does not prove a harbour view, garden frontage, level access, quietness, parking, a short walk or a particular street condition.

## Everyday life and public services

A public edition may use these as practical civic anchors, but should leave current office hours, collection arrangements and service contacts on the live council page.

Primary HealthCare locates Floriana Health Centre on Triq Frangisk Saver Fenech and currently describes a 24/7 immediate-care function alongside scheduled clinics. The public page should identify the centre and link the live service page without freezing individual clinic timetables, eligibility, appointment rules or telephone details into evergreen copy.

Malta Public Transport's live system shows Floriani A, B and C stops on multiple corridors. Route numbers and times change, so the edition should tell readers to check the exact stop, date and direction in the operator's live planner rather than publish a permanent timetable or journey time. “Valletta” departures may physically pass through Floriana, but that does not erase the locality boundary.

## Activities and heritage

The Floriana Local Council describes the Granaries at Pjazza San Publju as historic grain-storage pits and a large open urban space. That supports a heritage-orientation section, not a promise about current events, unrestricted access, noise, parking or conditions on a particular day.

The University of Malta operates the Argotti Botanic Gardens & Resource Centre in Floriana. Its current page identifies the national herbarium, Mediterranean and other botanical collections, teaching and research. Access, fees, events and opening arrangements should remain linked to the live University source.

Din l-Art Ħelwa manages the former Msida Bastion Cemetery as the Msida Bastion Historic Garden / Garden of Rest. The site is within Floriana despite overlooking the Marsamxett side. The public edition may name it as a separate heritage visit but must not copy an old timetable or infer that every nearby home has the same view or access.

- Floriana Local Council, Granaries: https://www.florianalocalcouncil.com/2017/04/03/granaries/

## Lightweight 2D map and route guardrails

A future page should use a fast, click-to-load OpenStreetMap frame centred broadly on Floriana. It may orient the reader to the locality only after consent. It must not draw an invented municipal boundary, property pin, straight connector, walking route, drive route, ferry line, distance or journey time.

Floriana's bastions, arterial roads, grade changes and garden walls make straight-line routing especially misleading. Any later exact-endpoint route must use real network geometry and distinguish entrances, crossings and direction. No harbour crossing may be shown as a walk. A ferry connection belongs only to a separately verified ferry endpoint and water segment; there is no basis in this dossier for drawing one through Floriana.
