## Editorial boundary and naming

Use **Ix-Xagħra, Gozo** at first mention and **Xagħra** thereafter. The Office of the Address Registrar lists official locality, local-council and regional names, while the Local Government Division places Xagħra Local Council in the Gozo Region. This is a separate locality from **Ix-Xgħajra, Malta**; the similar names must never be merged in copy, filters, metadata or search aliases.

Xagħra Local Council describes the settlement as occupying a hill in north-eastern central Gozo, less than four kilometres from Ir-Rabat. Its own historical outline distinguishes the older sectors ta’ Sant’Anton/tal-Qaċċa, the central Xagħret il-Għażżenin and the eastern tan-Nazzarenu, formerly ta’ Gajdoru. Those names can explain internal texture, but they do not establish a listing's exact quarter without an address-level source.

Keep Ir-Rabat/Victoria, Nadur, Marsalforn and the Ramla area editorially separate. References to nearby valleys, bays or neighbouring towns are regional orientation only. A `gozo:xaghra` taxonomy key and shared locality-centroid coordinates do not prove a property's street, view, walking conditions or proximity to a landmark.
