# St Paul's Bay edition — sourced research draft

Status: RESEARCH DRAFT. No public page, navigation entry or sitemap entry exists yet.

Last source review: 11 October 2026 (Europe/Malta).

## Editorial boundary

The public inventory currently contains six listable homes labelled St Paul's Bay. This is tied with Għargħur for the largest locality that does not yet have its own edition, so St Paul's Bay is the next research candidate. The label must refer to the St Paul's Bay town and harbour context around the parish/Gillieru side of the bay, rather than silently absorbing the whole local-council territory.

- **The old town and parish/harbour-facing streets** form the most recognisable St Paul's Bay context. An address here does not by itself prove a sea view, level access or a short route to the water.
- **The inland streets towards Mosta Road** have a different everyday setting and need address-specific checks.
- **Buġibba, Qawra, Xemxija and Burmarrad** fall within the same large local council according to the council, but remain separately named places. Qawra already has its own 2906 edition; the new page must not reuse that text or present every council area as St Paul's Bay town.

The [San Pawl il-Baħar Local Council geography page](https://sanpawlilbaharlc.gov.mt/en/homepage/geography-and-history/) explicitly lists Burmarrad, Qawra, Buġibba, Xemxija, Mselliet, San Martin and parts of other areas within its council. That evidence supports a wide administrative territory, not an interchangeable property neighbourhood. Malta's [Office of the Address Registrar](https://address.gov.mt/locate-a-street-search/) remains the authority for checking the locality attached to a specific street.

Do not promise a harbour view, quiet street, parking, bathing access, level walking or a fixed journey time. Those depend on the exact building, entrance, terrain, present access and transport conditions.

## Facts safe for evergreen copy

- The [Local Council geography and history page](https://sanpawlilbaharlc.gov.mt/en/homepage/geography-and-history/) places St Paul's Bay on Malta's north-east coast and distinguishes the council's many named areas. Its distance and population wording must not be turned into a current commute time or population statistic.
- The council's [historical-places directory](https://sanpawlilbaharlc.gov.mt/en/historical-places/) locates Wignacourt Tower and the Parish of Our Lady of Sorrows in St Paul's Bay while assigning other places to Qawra, Burmarrad, Salina and Xemxija. This supports keeping those contexts distinct. Opening, access and event details still need a current operator check.
- The council's [churches and chapels directory](https://sanpawlilbaharlc.gov.mt/en/churches-chapels/) gives separate addresses for the Our Lady of Sorrows Church and St Paul's Shipwrecked Church in St Paul's Bay. They are orientation references, not property-level distance evidence.
- [Primary HealthCare's health-centre directory](https://primaryhealthcare.gov.mt/en/health-centers/) assigns St Paul's Bay to Mosta Health Centre. Services, opening arrangements and the correct point of care can change and must be checked at the time of use.
- A [Servizz.gov announcement](https://www.gov.mt/en/Government/DOI/Press%20Releases/Pages/2023/11/29/pr231875en.aspx) records that a St Paul's Bay hub next to the parish church had opened by November 2023. The edition may use this as a dated public-service reference, not as a guarantee of current hours or services.

## Everyday life and activity candidates

- [SPAR Malta's operator locator](https://spar.com.mt/all-stores/) lists SPAR St Paul's Bay on Triq San Pawl. It is a directly sourced grocery example, not proof that it is closest to every listing; hours, stock and delivery remain current-check items.
- [Tide Bistro](https://tidebistro.mt/) gives its address as 66 Church Street, St Paul's Bay and describes a seaside bistro in the historic Gillieru setting. It can serve as one operator-backed dining example without reproducing reviews, rankings, mutable hours or price claims.
- [Gillieru Kafé's operator page](https://bastions.mt/gillieru-kafe/) locates the café beside Gillieru Harbour and St Paul's Shipwreck Church. It is a second operator-backed example for the harbour context, not a quality ranking or a complete local directory.
- The council's historical directory can support a carefully worded heritage section around Wignacourt Tower and the parish area. It must not imply that the sites share one route or are always open.

These are examples, not rankings. Qawra's aquarium/coast material and Buġibba's entertainment context must not be recycled as if they described every St Paul's Bay address.

## Map and mobility rules

- Use a fast click-to-load 2D OpenStreetMap overview centred on St Paul's Bay town. It may show neighbouring labels for orientation but must not draw an invented district boundary.
- Do not connect opposite sides of the bay or nearby coastal points with a straight line. No visible proximity can be presented as a walking route.
- Keep verified walking geometry, bus information, current road-traffic estimates and taxi-price observations as separate sourced layers.
- Do not infer a ferry service from the bay. A ferry label requires current route-specific operator or authority evidence and matching routed geometry.
- A property page may state a travel time only after a route request for that property's verified coordinates and the relevant mode.
