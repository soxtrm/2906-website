English editorial copy may introduce the place as **L-Isla (Senglea)** and use Senglea thereafter, but it must not flatten L-Isla, Bormla/Cospicua and Il-Birgu/Vittoriosa into one administrative “Three Cities” place. Kalkara also remains separate across the harbour context.

- Address Registrar, current locality table: https://address.gov.mt/localities/

1. the Xatt Juan B. Azopardo waterfront and marina edge;
2. the Triq il-Vitorja civic and basilica spine;

## Heritage and activities without access promises

Heritage Malta's published Senglea trail describes a sequence beginning at the marina, continuing to the Gardiola, the Palaces of the Captains of the Galleys and St Philip's Church, then through secondary roads to the Senglea Basilica. That past event is useful evidence for distinct urban contexts, not a current bookable tour, a step-free route or a property walking itinerary.

## Ferry and route guardrails

Valletta Ferry Services publishes a Valletta–Three Cities service whose operator schedule names **Cospicua** as the Three Cities terminal. It does not name a Senglea departure. Any future route presentation must therefore keep two modes separate:

1. a provider-routed land journey from a verified L-Isla origin to the Cospicua ferry terminal; then
2. the operator-confirmed ferry leg between Cospicua and Valletta.

The ferry cannot be represented as departing from an unspecified Senglea property. A straight line over Dockyard Creek or Grand Harbour must never be labelled as walking or driving. If exact endpoints and routed street geometry cannot be verified, show no route rather than an illustrative shortcut.

- Valletta Ferry Services, current schedule: https://www.vallettaferryservices.com/schedule/

Current operation, fare, schedule and access remain live operator checks. No private water taxi, fixed crossing time, continuous service or step-free interchange is claimed.
