## Name, administration and editorial boundary

Use the official Maltese name **Il-Qala** in titles and first mention, with **Qala** where grammar or an operator's own name requires it. The village is on eastern Gozo. Keep it distinct from Għajnsielem and Mġarr, Nadur, Comino and the wider coast. A property carrying the inventory locality label is not automatically in the square, at the belvedere or near Ħondoq ir-Rummien.

The official Local Council site is the administration source. Visit Gozo supplies locality and visitor context, including the village's eastern setting, Qala Belvedere, Madonna tal-Blat and the coast. These sources support editorial orientation only. They do not establish a particular street, view, gradient, parking arrangement, nearest stop, walking route or access condition for the live listing.
