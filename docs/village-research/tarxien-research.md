## Heritage and activities

Heritage Malta describes four megalithic structures from the late Neolithic period, subsequently reused during the Early Bronze Age. A visit can focus on the contrast between surviving architecture and the decorated South structure. The operator explains that vulnerable original finds were moved to the National Museum of Archaeology in Valletta. Avoid implying that every photographed original remains on site.

The operator reports a wheelchair-accessible walkway. Attribute this specifically to the site's published information and link its current access statement; do not generalise it to all approach streets. Link live opening/admission information instead of copying ticket prices, offers, times or guaranteed tour availability.

- [Complex and access information](https://heritagemalta.mt/explore/hal-tarxien-prehistoric-complex/)
