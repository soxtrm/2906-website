## Bay identity and council boundary

Xlendi is a coastal destination in southwestern Gozo, with a small beach, waterfront restaurants and cliffs beside the bay. Visit Gozo describes its fishing-village origins and the 1650 coastal tower. Keep the bay, tower and inland village of Munxar distinct in the edition. The tower is a separate destination from the waterfront; an orientation pin near the bus terminus establishes no route to it. [Visit Gozo · Xlendi](https://visitgozo.com/places/xlendi/)

The Munxar guide explicitly places Xlendi under Munxar Local Council. An Xlendi edition can therefore describe its own waterfront experience without presenting it as a separate local council. The council office is on Profs. Ġuże’ Aquilina Street in inland Munxar. Refer readers to its official notices and service pages rather than presenting the office as a waterfront amenity. [Visit Gozo · Munxar](https://visitgozo.com/places/il-munxar/) · [Munxar Local Council](https://munxarlc.gov.mt/en/) · [Council office](https://munxarlc.gov.mt/en/contact-us/)
