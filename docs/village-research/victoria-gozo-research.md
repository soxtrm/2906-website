## Editorial boundary and naming

Use **Ir-Rabat (Victoria), Gozo** at first mention and **Victoria** thereafter. The Office of the Address Registrar lists `Ir-Rabat (Għawdex)` in the Gozo Region, separately from `Ir-Rabat (Malta)`. The official Gozo Album and the local council explain that Victoria embraces both Iċ-Ċittadella and Ir-Rabat, its surrounding town/suburb. A loose `Rabat` match must never merge the Gozo city with Rabat on Malta.

The edition must also avoid absorbing neighbouring Fontana. Fontana Local Council describes its settlement as having developed on the hillside south of Ir-Rabat; that adjacency is not a licence to relabel Fontana homes or services as central Victoria. Likewise, a listing's `gozo:victoria` taxonomy key and shared locality-centroid coordinates do not establish its exact street, view, walking conditions or proximity to Iċ-Ċittadella, It-Tokk or the bus terminal.

## Heritage and place character

The official Gozo Album states that Victoria is Gozo's only city, that the name and city status date to 10 June 1887, and that the city includes both Iċ-Ċittadella and Ir-Rabat. Visit Gozo identifies Pjazza Indipendenza, known as It-Tokk, as the centre of Ir-Rabat and treats Iċ-Ċittadella as a distinct fortified precinct overlooking the surrounding town.

Heritage Malta currently identifies the Gozo Museum of Archaeology, Gran Castello Historic House and the Old Prison within Iċ-Ċittadella. Its March 2026 museum leaflet also records that the Gozo Museum of Archaeology closed on 13 April 2026 and that part of the Gozo Nature Museum closed on 16 March 2026. A future edition must therefore recheck the live status of every museum immediately before publication rather than copy an old attraction list or imply that all interiors are open.

The Citadel can anchor the heritage section, while It-Tokk and the streets of Ir-Rabat anchor the lived town. Do not turn those anchors into a single prescribed walking route. No gate access, opening time, ticket, surface condition, accessibility claim or journey duration belongs in the edition without a current source and an exact endpoint.
