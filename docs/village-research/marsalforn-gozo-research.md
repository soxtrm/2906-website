## Editorial boundary and naming

Use **Marsalforn, Gozo** at first mention and **Marsalforn** thereafter. The Environmental Health Directorate identifies Marsalforn Bay within the Żebbuġ, Gozo local-council area. Marsalforn is therefore a distinct coastal settlement and editorial subject, but it is not a separate local council. Keep it separate from the hilltop village of Żebbuġ, Victoria, Xagħra, Qbajjar, Xwejni and Għar Qawqla.

Visit Gozo describes Marsalforn as a bay settlement whose activity changes strongly between summer and winter. That supports an honest seasonal section; it does not justify a claim that every street is lively, quiet, tourist-facing or close to the shore. Qbajjar and Xwejni are west of Marsalforn and must remain separate coast contexts rather than being folded into the centre.

The current ARGUS record exposes only the locality centroid **36.0642, 14.2578** under the API's `locality centroid only` coordinate policy. It does not establish a street, building, bay frontage, view, beach access, gradient, parking arrangement, nearest stop or walking route. Do not reuse listing language as an area-wide claim.
