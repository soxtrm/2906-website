# Marsaskala edition — sourced research draft

Status: RESEARCH DRAFT. No public neighbourhood edition has been created or announced.

Last source review: 11 October 2026 (Europe/Malta).

## Editorial boundary

Marsaskala should be presented as a southern coastal locality with several different living contexts, rather than as one interchangeable seafront strip.

- **Marsaskala Bay and the harbour-facing streets** provide the clearest waterfront and promenade context.
- **The inland residential streets** behind the bay need address-specific checks; a Marsaskala address does not prove a sea view, flat access or a short walk to the waterfront.
- **St Thomas Bay and the Żonqor side** are distinct coastal contexts. They must not be collapsed into the central harbour or joined with an inferred walking line.

Malta's Office of the Address Registrar lists Marsaskala as its own locality and local council in the Southern Region. The council's beach page separately names St Thomas Bay, Wara ż-Żonqor Bay, Żonqor Bay and Blue Hole (Żonqor), which supports treating those coastal areas as separate orientation cues. Neither source defines a property-level neighbourhood boundary.

Do not promise a harbour view, bathing access, easy parking, a quiet street, level walking or a fixed journey time. Those depend on the exact building, entrance, terrain, current access and transport conditions.

## Facts safe for evergreen copy

- [Marsaskala Local Council](https://marsaskalalc.gov.mt/) describes the locality's sheltered harbour and its development from a small fishing port into a year-round settlement. Historical and undated population wording must not be presented as a current statistic.
- The council's [beaches page](https://marsaskalalc.gov.mt/en/beaches/) separately identifies St Thomas Bay, Wara ż-Żonqor Bay, Żonqor Bay and Blue Hole (Żonqor). It supports coastal orientation only; it is not evidence of current water quality, accessibility or a route from a property.
- Malta's [Office of the Address Registrar](https://address.gov.mt/localities/) lists Marsaskala in the Southern Region. This establishes the administrative locality but does not justify drawing an invented district boundary.
- Primary HealthCare's [community-clinic directory](https://primaryhealthcare.gov.mt/en/clinics/malta-community-clinics/) lists Marsaskala Community Clinic at Sant Anna Garden. Its [health-centre directory](https://primaryhealthcare.gov.mt/en/health-centers/) places Marsaskala in the Cospicua Health Centre catchment. Services and schedules remain mutable and should stay behind the official source.

## Everyday life and activity candidates

- [The Convenience Shop's operator locator](https://my.mt/locations/) identifies MySupermarket Marsascala at Triq il-Qaliet and a MyConvenience branch at Triq Sant' Antnin, Wied il-Għajn. These are operator-backed grocery examples; hours, stock, delivery and distance from a listing require a current check.
- [SPAR Malta's operator locator](https://spar.com.mt/all-stores/) identifies EUROSPAR Marsaskala at Triq in-Nadur. It is another weekly-shopping example, not a claim that it is closest to every property.
- [Roti](https://roti.mt/) gives its Marsaskala address at Triq ix-Xatt, corner with Triq Sant' Antnin, and describes its own Indian-food offer. It can serve as one operator-backed dining example without reproducing rankings, discounts or mutable opening details.
- St Thomas Bay and the other council-listed coastal sites can be described as orientation and outdoor-life contexts only. Current bathing status must be checked through the competent authority at the time of use and must not be frozen into evergreen copy.

These entries are examples, not rankings or a complete local directory. Operator spelling alternates between Marsaskala, Marsascala and Wied il-Għajn; the edition should preserve the official locality label while quoting exact venue addresses accurately.

## Map and mobility rules

- Use a fast click-to-load 2D OpenStreetMap overview that can show the central bay, inland residential fabric, St Thomas Bay and the Żonqor side without drawing a false border.
- Do not connect the coastal contexts with a straight line or infer a waterfront walking route from their visual proximity.
- Keep verified walking geometry, bus information, current road-traffic estimates and taxi-price observations as separate sourced layers.
- Do not infer a ferry service. Any future ferry label requires a current, route-specific operator or authority source and matching routed geometry.
- A property page may state a travel time only after a route request for that property's verified coordinates and the relevant mode.
