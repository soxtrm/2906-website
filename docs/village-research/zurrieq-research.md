Language: English. Status: local noindex preview verified; unpublished. Checked 18 October 2026 (Europe/Malta).

## Why this locality is next

The public 2906 inventory endpoint contained six available, image-bearing listings labelled exactly `Żurrieq` or `Zurrieq` at the time of review. After normalising those two spellings and excluding the 22 localities already represented in the public neighbourhood sitemap, Żurrieq was the largest clearly distinct unpublished locality label. ## Editorial boundary

The edition must distinguish the main Żurrieq town from Bubaqra and in-Nigret, which the Local Council identifies as two hamlets lying away from the main centre. Ħal Millieri is a peripheral Żurrieq context with historic chapels. Those names can add useful texture without being presented as interchangeable micro-locations or exact property boundaries.

Qrendi remains a separate property-search and municipal context. Qrendi Local Council states that Wied iż-Żurrieq and the Blue Grotto have been within Qrendi's council limits since the Local Councils Act of 1993, despite the valley's name. The edition must therefore avoid using Blue Grotto, Wied iż-Żurrieq, Ħaġar Qim or Mnajdra as if they were attractions inside Żurrieq town or evidence that a Żurrieq listing has coastal access.

## Heritage and activities

Żurrieq Local Council identifies the Xarolla windmill as a 1724 windmill restored in 1992 and administered by the council since 2000 as a cultural centre. The same council page describes Bubaqra Tower and the historic windmills, while its geography page identifies the parish church and the Ħal Millieri chapels. These are suitable locality anchors, but the eventual page must not promise opening, internal access, demonstrations or event availability without a fresh operator or council check.

- Council historical places: https://zurrieqlc.gov.mt/en/historical-places/
- Council geography and history: https://zurrieqlc.gov.mt/en/homepage/geography-and-history/
- Government record of completed Wardija Tower restoration: https://www.gov.mt/en/Government/DOI/Press%20Releases/Pages/2022/06/01/pr220722en.aspx
