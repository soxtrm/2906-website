## A village square inland from Xlendi

Visit Gozo places Munxar between two valleys and describes its church, dedicated to St Paul, as the village centre. It dates the church’s construction to 1914–1925 and parish independence to December 1957. Its locality page also records wartime shelters beneath the square, constructed in 1942. Treat those shelters as documented heritage; current visitor access has not been established. [Visit Gozo · Munxar](https://visitgozo.com/places/il-munxar/)

The tourism authority’s January 2026 feature distinguishes the inland village core from its coastal connections at Sanap, Ras il-Bajjada and Xlendi. Use the parish square, valley setting and local services to give this edition its own focus, rather than repeating the Xlendi waterfront brochure. The same feature identifies the church’s St Paul statue as a 1933 work by Wistin Camilleri. [Visit Gozo · village and coast](https://visitgozo.com/2026/01/23/munxar-gozo-a-quiet-village-between-valleys-and-coast/)

The two official tourism pages differ in how precisely they place the May village festa: one gives the third Sunday, the newer article describes the end of the month. Omit a fixed future festa date unless the parish or council publishes a dated programme. Do not turn either historic description into a current event listing.
