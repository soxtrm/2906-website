## Village, conservation area and archaeological park

- The Address Registrar identifies Il-Qrendi as a separate locality in the Western Region. Centre the edition on the parish village, with separate visitor information for the temples and coast. The Parish Church of the Assumption is heritage inventory item 01778. The council describes Qrendi’s earlier ecclesiastical link to Żurrieq. Confirm interior access with the parish if including a visit. [Localities register](https://address.gov.mt/localities/), [National inventory](https://parlament.mt/media/126246/02958.pdf), [Qrendi council churches and chapels](https://qrendilc.gov.mt/en/churches-chapels/)
- Il-Maqluba is a designated Special Area of Conservation. St Matthew’s church, inventory item 01788, is at Misraħ tal-Maqluba. ERA conservation measures restrict access to the sinkhole floor. Cover the place through its landscape and above-ground setting. Keep specific access advice tied to posted restrictions and omit any descent route. [ERA protected-areas list](https://era.org.mt/list-of-protected-areas/), [ERA conservation notice](https://era.org.mt/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/GN_1372_of_2016.pdf), [St Matthew’s inventory sheet](https://schmalta.mt/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/DC-01788.pdf)
- Ħaġar Qim and Mnajdra are distinct late-Neolithic sites within the same managed archaeological park in Qrendi. They form part of UNESCO’s serial Megalithic Temples of Malta inscription. Heritage Malta describes an internal footpath between the two sites and different accessibility arrangements. Link the operator’s current visit planner for admission, hours and access. Any map route should use verified path geometry. [Heritage Malta](https://heritagemalta.mt/explore/hagar-qim-and-mnajdra-archaeological-park/), [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/132/)
- Blue Grotto cave excursions depart from the quay at Wied iż-Żurrieq and run according to conditions determined by the Blue Grotto Boat Service. Describe this as a sightseeing boat excursion. The locality map should stop at the shore and carry no implied ferry, walking or swimming connection. Distinguish the quay, the viewpoint and Żurrieq’s village centre. [Boat operator](https://www.bluegrottomalta.com.mt/about-us), [Quay project location on the ERA record](https://era.org.mt/era-project/pa05472-24/)
