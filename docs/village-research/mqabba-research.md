# L-Imqabba neighbourhood edition

Private research draft, checked 16 November 2026. Planned language: English. Intended public URL: https://www.2906.estate/link-marketplace/areas/mqabba.html (HTTP 404 at the source check).

## Editorial focus

Start at Pjazza tal-Knisja. Contrast the everyday parish square and the walk to Triq San Bażilju with the settlement’s limestone-quarrying history. The council describes how the local soft limestone shaped building and employment. The April 2026 public-works notice confirms completed restoration of the main square and a maintenance agreement. It separately describes an agreement for lighting St Catherine’s Chapel. This is not confirmation that the lighting is installed or that the chapel is open. [Address Registrar: Western Region](https://address.gov.mt/localities/) · [Mqabba Local Council](https://mqabbalc.gov.mt/en/) · [April 2026 public-works notice](https://www.gov.mt/en/Government/DOI/Press%20Releases/Pages/2026/04/24/pr260729en.aspx)

The parish church is dedicated to the Assumption. St Basil’s and St Michael’s are separate chapels near one another. The council describes the significance of the earlier St Basil parish site. Use exterior photos as orientation and link to the parish for visiting arrangements. [Archdiocese parish listing](https://parishes.church.mt/parish/imqabba/) · [Council churches and chapels](https://mqabbalc.gov.mt/en/churches-chapels/)

Heritage Malta manages the Ta’ Mintna Catacombs. Its visitor information limits entry to prior appointment with staff. Treat this as a planned heritage visit rather than an open public shortcut or a drop-in attraction. Quarrying belongs in the village’s historical context, with no visitor recommendation for private works or quarry edges. [Heritage Malta](https://heritagemalta.mt/explore/ta-mintna-catacombs/) · [Council history](https://mqabbalc.gov.mt/en/)

## Everyday life

- Malta Public Transport shows route 72 on the Valletta–Qrendi corridor and route 218 between Mqabba and Mater Dei. Both timetables list Mqabba stops, including Mentna and Bazilju on the checked direction. Route 72 also carried a construction detour notice at the time of verification. Link the operator's current directional timetable and service notices. Leave exact times out of the edition. [Route 72](https://www.publictransport.com.mt/route/72/) · [Route 218](https://www.publictransport.com.mt/route/218/) · [Service notices](https://www.publictransport.com.mt/latest-service-updates/)
