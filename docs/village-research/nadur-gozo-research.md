## The square and the ridge

Nadur Local Council uses In-Nadur for the eastern Gozo locality. The guide can orient readers around the church and square dedicated to Saints Peter and Paul, then give Ta’ Kenuna and the coastal landscape their own context. Visit Gozo identifies Ta’ Kenuna as a nineteenth-century communications tower built in 1848 and describes the surrounding botanical garden. Use these as landscape and heritage stops. Current interior access and garden hours need confirmation. Keep Il-Qala, Għajnsielem and the Mġarr ferry terminal as distinct destinations. [Nadur Local Council](https://nadurlc.gov.mt/en/) · [Visit Gozo, In-Nadur](https://visitgozo.com/places/in-nadur/) · [Il-Qala edition](https://www.2906.estate/link-marketplace/areas/qala-gozo.html) · [Għajnsielem edition](https://www.2906.estate/link-marketplace/areas/ghajnsielem.html)

Visit Gozo describes Ta’ Isopu and the coastline between San Blas and Daħlet Qorrot. Distinguish that coastal setting from the village square and Ta’ Kenuna. The official walking leaflet offers landscape context, but its age means route conditions need a separate check before publishing directions. [Visit Gozo, In-Nadur](https://visitgozo.com/places/in-nadur/) · [Visit Gozo walking leaflet](https://explore.visitgozo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/HondoqWalk.pdf)

## Daily life, food and transport

- Mekren’s Bakery describes wood-fired ftira, pizza and qassatat from its Nadur bakery. Visit Gozo gives separate context for Maxokk in the village core and its focus on ftira and pizza. Use the bakery sources for current collection, hours and menus. [Mekren’s Bakery](https://mekrensbakery.com/) · [Visit Gozo bakery guide](https://visitgozo.com/2024/04/24/on-the-trail-of-gozos-old-bakeries/)
- The Quaint Collection describes The Fat Rabbit as local and Mediterranean cooking and identifies it as the restaurant at its Nadur location. Link to the operator for opening and booking details. [The Fat Rabbit](https://www.quaintcollection.com/the-fat-rabbit/) · [Quaint Nadur](https://www.quaintcollection.com/hotel-nadur/)
- Malta Public Transport lists stop Nadur 1723 on route 303 and includes In-Nadur on route 322. Check dates and directions with the journey planner. For an inter-island trip, keep the Gozo land transfer to Mġarr and the Gozo Channel ferry to Ċirkewwa as separate modes. The operator home page identifies the terminals. [Route 303](https://www.publictransport.com.mt/route/303/) · [Route 322](https://www.publictransport.com.mt/route/322/) · [Journey planner](https://www.publictransport.com.mt/journey-planner/) · [Gozo Channel](https://www.gozochannel.com/)
- Primary HealthCare publishes the Victoria Health Centre service details for Gozo. Confirm the service and appointment location before travelling. [Victoria Health Centre](https://primaryhealthcare.gov.mt/en/health-centers/victoria-health-center/)
