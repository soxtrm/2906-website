## Editorial structure and boundaries

Malta's Address Registrar lists Baħar iċ-Ċagħaq under the local council of In-Naxxar in the Northern Region. A useful edition should distinguish three contexts rather than presenting one generic coastal promise:

1. the inland residential core, parish buildings and Baħar iċ-Ċagħaq Square;
2. the Coast Road edge, the official bathing-water monitoring point and Ġnien it-Tbissim;
3. the Qalet Marku coastal and historic-defence edge.

Naxxar, Magħtab, Madliena, Għargħur, Pembroke and Salina remain distinct locality or search contexts. The exact address and verified route come first.
