Il-Qrendi / Western Region

Qrendi, from village square to temple ridge

Begin at the Church of the Assumption. Beyond the village centre, St Matthew’s overlooks the Il-Maqluba conservation area, and Heritage Malta manages two Neolithic sites in a separate park.

The parish, Il-Maqluba and the temples

The national inventory lists the Church of the Assumption as item 01778. Qrendi’s council traces the village’s parish history through neighbouring Żurrieq. Start with the façade and square, and check parish arrangements before planning an interior visit.

Council parish history ↗

St Matthew’s at Misraħ tal-Maqluba

St Matthew’s church, national inventory item 01788, stands by a protected natural depression. Use the above-ground setting to appreciate how the buildings sit against the landscape. The conservation rules restrict access to the floor of Il-Maqluba. Follow on-site notices.

ERA protected areas ↗

Ħaġar Qim and Mnajdra

These are two distinct sites in the same archaeological park and part of UNESCO’s Megalithic Temples of Malta. Heritage Malta describes an internal path between them. Check the park’s visitor information for access, admission and the different terrain at each site.

Heritage Malta visitor information ↗

Blue Grotto by boat

The sightseeing boats leave from the quay at Wied iż-Żurrieq. The shore viewpoint, the quay and Żurrieq’s village centre are separate destinations. Check the weather and the operator’s notice before travelling to the quay.
