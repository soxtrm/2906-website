## Name, administration and editorial boundary

Use the official Maltese name **Ħal Luqa** in titles and first mention, with **Luqa** where an operator's own naming or a search label requires it. Malta's Address Registrar and local-government directory place Ħal Luqa in the Southern Region.

Keep the village centre, Malta International Airport and its aviation or business areas, and Ħal Farruġ as separate editorial contexts. The wider locality context does not make every Luqa home an “airport property”. Keep Ħal Luqa distinct from Gudja, Kirkop, Mqabba, Safi, Qormi, Marsa, Santa Luċija, Paola and the surrounding airport or industrial areas.

## Village core, parish and aviation history are different contexts

The Archdiocese maintains the current Ħal Luqa parish page for St Andrew's parish. This supports a concise heritage section about the parish and older village fabric. It does not establish that a current listing is in the village core, close to the church or on a particular street.

Transport Malta's civil-aviation history says that Luqa aerodrome opened as an all-weather field on 1 April 1940, that a new terminal was inaugurated in 1992, and that Malta International Airport took operational management on 1 May 1998. These dates support a separate aviation-history section. They do not establish the present-day noise, flight path, road access, travel time or view of any property.

Sources:

## Bus context without a frozen timetable

Malta Public Transport currently publishes Route 208 with Luqa and airport-corridor stops, including Azzopardi, Mitjar and Avjazzjoni. The route continues through other localities towards Marsa, University and Mater Dei. This establishes a live operator corridor only. It does not prove a guaranteed departure, frequency, connection, nearest stop, walking distance or property-specific journey time.

Airport Direct is a separate operator service and should remain labelled as airport transport. An airport terminal route must not be presented as proof that a listing is near the terminal or that the village core has the same service pattern.
