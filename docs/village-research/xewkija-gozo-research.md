## The village square and the mill

Ix-Xewkija lies in inland Gozo between the Mġarr harbour side and Ir-Rabat (Victoria). Its village centre is identifiable by the Rotunda of St John the Baptist. Visit Gozo highlights the limestone work inside the church. The parish publishes current notices. Use the square and Rotunda as orientation points and direct visitors to the parish for arrangements. Keep nearby Għajnsielem and Victoria as separate locality links. [Xewkija Local Council](https://xewkijalc.gov.mt/en/) · [Visit Gozo, Ix-Xewkija](https://visitgozo.com/places/ix-xewkija/) · [Xewkija parish](https://www.xewkijaparish.org/)

Tat-Tmien Kantunieri windmill is a second heritage stop on the main road. The Cultural Heritage Directorate describes it as Gozo’s oldest surviving windmill. The government newsletter places it at a Xewkija crossroads and records its commissioning under Grand Master Perellos. Keep its approach and any visiting arrangements separate from those of the Rotunda, and avoid implying that the mill is open on the day. [Cultural Heritage Directorate](https://culturalheritagegozo.gov.mt/project/the-xewkija-windmill/) · [Government heritage newsletter](https://malteselivingabroad.gov.mt/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/NEWS283.pdf)
