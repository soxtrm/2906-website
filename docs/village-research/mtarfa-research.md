## The ridge and the town square

L-Imtarfa has its own local council. Its first council election took place in 2000 after the locality had been administered by Rabat. Tell the story through the military ridge, the modern civic square and the parish. The Clock Tower dates to around 1895. The Government Gazette locates it at the corner of Triq it-Torri and Triq il-Gilju and records Grade 1 protection. The former military hospital on Triq l-Imtarfa is Grade 2 and now houses the Sir Temi Zammit school, according to the council. [Mtarfa Local Council](https://mtarfalc.gov.mt/en/) · [Council geography and history](https://mtarfalc.gov.mt/en/homepage/geography-and-history/) · [Government Gazette](https://www.gov.mt/en/Government/DOI/Government%20Gazette/Documents/2008/07/GG%2021.7.pdf)
