## Parish, valley and locality discipline

Kerċem Local Council places the village southwest of Victoria, between Lunzjata Valley and the hills of Tal-Mixta, Għar Ilma and Ta’ Dbieġi. Use the council’s locality framing: the Visit Gozo village page’s southeast-of-Victoria wording conflicts with it. Avoid repeating that direction. [Council](https://kercemlc.gov.mt/en/) · [Visit Gozo · Ta’ Kerċem](https://visitgozo.com/places/ta-kercem/)

The council’s churches page identifies the parish sanctuary’s joint dedication to Our Lady of Perpetual Help and Pope St Gregory the Great. It records completion in 1851, parish status in 1885, and enlargement in 1906–1910. The same source identifies the Annunciation chapel at Lunzjata and approaches to the valley from Kerċem or Fontana. Keep the parish square and valley chapel separate. [Council churches and chapels](https://kercemlc.gov.mt/en/churches-chapels/)

Visit Gozo describes Lunzjata as an agricultural valley with springs and an Annunciation chapel near its entrance. Its general accessibility label is not an audited step-free route from a listing. Link to the source for the landscape, and check a precise approach and current conditions before offering access advice. [Official valley guide](https://visitgozo.com/places/il-lunzjata-valley/)

The broader locality description includes countryside toward San Rafflu and the western coast. Santa Luċija here is the Gozo hamlet within the Kerċem area, not the Malta town already covered at `/areas/santa-lucija.html`. Do not route searches for the Gozo hamlet to that Malta edition. Use specific destination names rather than a generic locality-wide walking time.
