## Editorial boundary and naming

Use **Ix-Xgħajra** at first mention and **Xgħajra** thereafter. The Office of the Address Registrar lists Ix-Xgħajra as a separate local council in the Port Region. The council describes it as a coastal locality bordering Żabbar and the SmartCity/Kalkara side. This supports a bounded locality identity; it does not turn council promotional language or old population figures into current housing claims.

Keep Xgħajra distinct from Żabbar, Kalkara, SmartCity, Marsaskala, Żonqor and the wider Inwadar coast. Żabbar is a separate inland town, Kalkara has a separate council and creek/Grand Harbour setting, and SmartCity is a named development context rather than another name for Xgħajra. The promenade, compact built area, battery setting and more open coast towards Inwadar are different contexts and must not be presented as one uniform neighbourhood experience.

The single current ARGUS record discloses only the shared locality centroid **35.8853, 14.5450** under the API's `locality centroid only` coordinate policy. It does not establish a street, building, seafront position, promenade proximity, view, gradient, parking arrangement, nearest stop or access route. Do not reuse listing language as an area-wide claim.

The Local Council's current history page identifies Della Grazie Battery as a landmark, explains that the British battery developed around the earlier Santa Maria delle Grazie tower site, and says the battery is in the council's care with restoration intended around a public-space project. That source supports a restrained heritage anchor and the battery's role in the settlement story.

Restoration state, public access, opening arrangements and project completion are mutable.
