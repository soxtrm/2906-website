## Village square, craft workshops and coast

San Lawrenz is an inland village in western Gozo with Dwejra inside its council territory. Keep the square and parish church as the village point of reference. Use Dwejra as a separate excursion. The council lists its office on Lady of Sorrow Street. For local errands, Visit Gozo places Ta’ Dbieġi Craft Village beside San Lawrenz, and its operator describes workshops and direct sales by artisans. Link out for current workshop hours and verify addresses individually around the Għarb boundary. [San Lawrenz Local Council](https://sanlawrenzlc.gov.mt/en/) · [Council services and address](https://sanlawrenzlc.gov.mt/en/contact-us/) · [Visit Gozo, San Lawrenz](https://visitgozo.com/places/san-lawrenz/) · [INDIS Malta, Ta’ Dbieġi](https://indismalta.com/zones/ta-dbiegi-artisan-village-malta/)

## Land and water at Dwejra

Dwejra’s Inland Sea is a seawater lagoon reached by land from Gozo. Visit Gozo describes its connection to the open sea and separately lists boat trips from the lagoon. Show the road or bus as the approach and label the boat as a separate optional activity. The water passage supplies no pedestrian connection. Visit Gozo records the collapse of the Azure Window in 2017. Fungus Rock and the Inland Sea remain distinct features and should have distinct labels when shown. Check boat availability or weather locally. [Visit Gozo, Dwejra](https://visitgozo.com/places/dwejra/) · [Visit Gozo boat directory](https://visitgozo.com/experience/boat-trips/) · [Gozo Album, Id-Dwejra](https://gozoalbum.gov.mt/localities/dwejra-26)

Din l-Art Ħelwa publishes visitor information for Dwejra Tower. Verify opening arrangements directly before describing interior access. The tourism walking leaflet is a source for landscape context. A current trail check is required before reusing its route as directions, and its historical depictions should be dated.
