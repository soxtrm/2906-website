## Editorial angle

Naxxar should be presented through its lived-in historic core around Victory Square, with Palazzo Parisio as a documented cultural anchor and ordinary shops and dining close to the centre. The edition must keep that core separate from the much larger Naxxar local-council territory, which also reaches coastal and rural areas including Salina, Maghtab and Bahar ic-Caghaq. A place being inside the municipality does not make it walkable from the village centre.

## Facts safe for evergreen copy

- Naxxar Local Council records evidence from several periods across the locality, including prehistoric and Bronze Age remains, Punic graves and catacombs. Because several cited sites sit outside the village core, these facts can establish the locality's long history but cannot support a compact walking itinerary. Source: https://naxxarlc.gov.mt/en/homepage/geography-and-history/
- The council's heritage material documents a broad and varied territory with coastal and inland sites, the Victoria Lines, towers and Il-Widna. The edition must identify an attraction's actual area instead of labelling every municipal site as central Naxxar. Source: https://naxxarlc.gov.mt/en/historical-places/
- Naxxar Local Council describes Palazzo Parisio and the Scicluna family's major alterations between 1898 and 1906. The palace operator places the site at 29 Victory Square and currently offers visits, gardens, dining and events. Mutable opening hours, prices and event dates must stay behind source links. Sources: https://naxxarlc.gov.mt/en/palaces/ and https://www.palazzoparisio.com/about-us and https://www.palazzoparisio.com/contact
- The council lists several public gardens and open spaces. These may support a later everyday-life section only after each exact entrance and current public access is checked; the current research does not turn them into a continuous route. Source: https://naxxarlc.gov.mt/en/open-spaces-gardens/

## Everyday life and food anchors

- EUROSPAR Naxxar is an operator-verified supermarket serving everyday groceries in Naxxar. The operator describes butcher/deli, bakery, fruit and vegetables and household essentials. Current hours and promotions are mutable and should remain links. Source: https://spar.com.mt/eurospar-naxxar/
- Arkadia Foodstore lists a Naxxar supermarket at 17–19 Labour Avenue. It is a second operator-backed everyday-shopping example, not a claim that every part of the municipality is equally served. Source: https://arkadiafoodstore.com/locations/
- Onella is an operator-verified wine bar and dining venue on Victory Square. Its own site says it opened in 2022 and focuses on wine and sharing food; marketing superlatives, bottle counts and opening hours should not be copied into evergreen text. Source: https://www.onella.mt/
- Banh Mi is an operator-verified Vietnamese and Asian restaurant at the Vittorja Band Club, 41–43 Triq Santa Lucija, Naxxar. Hours are variable and should remain linked. Source: https://banhmimalta.com/

These are examples with direct operator evidence, not rankings or exhaustive recommendations.

## Map and mobility boundary

Use a fast click-to-load 2D map centred on the Naxxar village core. It may orient the reader around Victory Square, Palazzo Parisio and verified everyday anchors, but must not draw a municipal boundary or imply that Salina, Maghtab, Bahar ic-Caghaq, Victoria Lines sites or other outlying council areas are a short walk away. Walking routes, bus timetables and live driving estimates remain separate. No straight line is a route, and no duration belongs in the edition without an exact routed endpoint pair.

## Release gates

1. Verify exact map anchors and public entrances independently; do not turn the council's broad heritage list into a village-centre trail.
2. Recheck all operator and council sources immediately before release; keep mutable prices, hours, menus and events out of evergreen copy.
3. Test the local noindex draft, image and click-to-load OSM overview on mobile and desktop before adding canonical, discovery and sitemap entries.
