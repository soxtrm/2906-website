Private research draft, 16 November 2026. Planned language is English and the planned canonical URL is https://www.2906.estate/link-marketplace/areas/safi.html. The URL returned HTTP 404 during research.

## The square and the walled garden

- The Address Registrar places Ħal Safi in Malta’s Western Region. Its location in the south of the main island is a geographic description. Ħal Kirkop and Żurrieq have separate locality entries and published editions. [Office of the Address Registrar](https://address.gov.mt/localities/)
- The parish church dedicated to St Paul stands on Misraħ il-Knisja and appears as item 01883 in the national heritage inventory. The council describes Safi’s historical connection to the Bir Miftuħ parish in Gudja. Use the church exterior and square to explain village orientation. The parish centre is a separate building discussed in a January 2026 work-in-progress announcement. Check current access before covering either interior or suggesting a visit during works. [Heritage inventory](https://parlament.mt/media/126246/02958.pdf), [Council churches and chapels](https://safilc.gov.mt/en/churches-chapels/), [Public Works update](https://www.gov.mt/en/Government/DOI/Press%20Releases/Pages/2026/01/12/PR260029en.aspx)
- Sir Alexander Ball’s Garden, also known as Ġnien tal-Kmand, is the second focus. The council describes a walled garden with a central path and trellis. The EU CAP Network records restoration of the garden’s fabric and support for its use as a community space. The council identifies it as open to the public, but check gates, hours and step-free access separately before making practical claims. [Local council heritage notes](https://safilc.gov.mt/en/historical-places/), [EU CAP Network garden project](https://eu-cap-network.ec.europa.eu/good-practice/restoration-and-embellishment-public-gardens-maltese-village_en)

## Everyday planning

- Safi falls within the Kirkop Health Centre catchment. The January 2026 service charter lists a community clinic at Dar il-Kunsill, School Street. The current community-clinic web directory lacks a matching Safi entry. Publish the catchment and service links, and leave the local clinic’s current operation and times unresolved. [Primary HealthCare service charter](https://primaryhealthcare.gov.mt/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/January-2026-Quality-Service-Charter.pdf), [Current clinics directory](https://primaryhealthcare.gov.mt/en/clinics/malta-community-clinics/), [Kirkop centre](https://primaryhealthcare.gov.mt/en/health-centers/kirkop-health-center/)
- Malta Public Transport’s route 73 timetable includes Ħal Safi, with Midri and Puniċi shown in the operator’s HTML. Stops and detours are direction- and date-dependent. Link the operator timetable and omit static times. [Route 73](https://www.publictransport.com.mt/route/73/)
- Il Mulino Cafe & Wine Bar appears in the MTA town-filtered bar register as **BAR-15953**, Triq il-Menqa. Town-filter checks returned HTTP 200 for bars, restaurants, snack bars and takeaways. No exact SAFI row was found in the latter three responses. The bar record verifies the establishment name, category and recorded address. Confirm trading, hours and menus directly before making recommendations. [MTA bar register, Safi filter](https://mta.com.mt/licences/licences/?town=SAFI&size=250&type=bars)
