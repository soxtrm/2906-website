## Editorial angle

Mellieħa needs to be shown as several distinct contexts rather than one compact resort label. The hillside village core around the parish church and sanctuary is separate from Mellieħa Bay and Għadira Nature Reserve below it, while the Red Tower, Marfa, Ċirkewwa, Aħrax and Manikata sit farther out in the wider locality. The future edition should help a renter understand those differences without presenting the entire local-council area as a single walkable district.

## Facts safe for evergreen copy

- The Office of the Address Registrar lists Il-Mellieħa in Malta's Northern Region. Mellieħa Local Council's own places-of-interest material separately identifies the parish church and sanctuary, Għadira Nature Reserve, the Red Tower, the White Tower, Manikata sites and other outlying landmarks. That separation supports a locality-wide overview, but not a claim that all of those places are close together. Sources: https://address.gov.mt/localities/ and https://melliehalc.gov.mt/en/about-locality/
- Mellieħa Local Council's history material documents the sanctuary complex and a church presence recorded in 1436, with later rebuilding and parish development. The Archdiocese describes the icon as probably Byzantine-Sicilian from around the eleventh century and records the site's recognition as a National Shrine in 2015. Religious origin stories must be labelled as tradition rather than presented as independently established history. Sources: https://melliehahistory.mt/sanctuary-museum/ and https://church.mt/doors-of-mercy/the-national-shrine-of-our-lady-of-mellieha/
- BirdLife Malta describes Għadira as a seven-hectare brackish lake and saltmarsh on the outskirts of Mellieħa, protected as a bird sanctuary, Ramsar site and Special Area of Conservation. BirdLife says around 140 migrant bird species are seen there annually. Its seasonal opening period and current visiting hours are mutable and should remain behind the operator link. Source: https://birdlifemalta.org/nature-reserves/ghadira/
- Din l-Art Ħelwa places St Agatha's Tower, commonly called the Red Tower, on Marfa Ridge overlooking Għadira Bay, the reserve and the channel toward Comino and Gozo. It belongs in an outlying-landmarks section, not in a village-centre walking itinerary. Current access, hours and fees must be checked immediately before publication. Source: https://www.static.dinlarthelwa.org/heritage-sites/managed-heritage-sites/st-agathas-tower-the-red-tower-mellieha/

- one80's operator site identifies its Mellieħa restaurant at 30 Triq il-Wied ta' Ruman and describes a Mediterranean menu with vegetarian and vegan choices. Mutable menus, hours and promotional language should not be copied into evergreen text. Source: https://one80.com.mt/mellieha/
- Rebekah's operator site identifies the restaurant at 12 Triq it-Tgħam and describes its setting in a renovated house of character and use of local produce. This can support a food example without repeating awards, rankings or superlatives. Source: https://rebekahsrestaurant.com/index.html
- Square Gastro Bar's official site identifies a venue on Parish Square, providing a directly sourced village-core dining example. Da Mello's official contact page identifies its restaurant at 120 Triq il-Kbira in central Mellieħa. These are examples, not a best-of list. Sources: https://squarebar.com.mt/ and https://damello.com.mt/contact/

The release should distinguish village-core shopping and dining from services near the bay or in the wider locality. It must not infer short walks, parking availability or year-round opening from an operator address alone.

## Map and mobility boundary

Use a fast click-to-load 2D OpenStreetMap overview centred on the hillside village core. The first view may orient the reader around Parish Square and the sanctuary, but it must not draw an invented locality boundary or a straight-line route. Mellieħa Bay/Għadira, the nature reserve, Red Tower/Marfa, Ċirkewwa, Aħrax and Manikata should be labelled as separate contexts or optional map targets rather than folded into a single pedestrian circuit.

Walking routes, bus schedules, live driving estimates and ferry connections remain separate datasets. No duration belongs in the brochure without an exact routed endpoint pair, source and timestamp.
