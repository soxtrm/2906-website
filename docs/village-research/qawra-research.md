# Qawra edition — sourced research draft

Status: release candidate verified on 10 October 2026. Publication is recorded only after the production URL passes live checks.

Last source review: 10 October 2026 (Europe/Malta).

## Editorial boundary

Qawra should be presented as a peninsula and coastal neighbourhood within the wider San Pawl il-Baħar urban area, not as a standalone council or as a synonym for every address around St Paul's Bay.

- **Qawra Point and the coast**, including the seafront and the aquarium area, form the clearest visitor-facing context.
- **The streets inside the peninsula**, including the area around Triq il-Merluzz, are a separate everyday residential context behind the coast.
- **Buġibba, old St Paul's Bay, Xemxija and the other San Pawl il-Baħar localities** remain distinct labels even though the built-up area is continuous in places.

Malta's Office of the Address Registrar lists `San Pawl il-Baħar` as the official locality and does not give Qawra a separate locality row. A listing therefore needs its own verified address before it is labelled Qawra. The planning document cited below helps explain the peninsula's physical relationship with Buġibba, but it is not a cadastral boundary and must not be used to draw one.

Do not promise a sea view, a quiet street, easy parking, beach access or blanket walkability. Those claims depend on the exact building, entrance, terrain and current road conditions.

## Facts safe for evergreen copy

- [San Pawl il-Baħar Local Council](https://sanpawlilbaharlc.gov.mt/en/) places Qawra within the connected urban area that also includes Buġibba, San Pawl, Xemxija and Burmarrad. Its history also dates Qawra Tower to 1637. Undated population figures should not be reused as current statistics.
- Malta's [Office of the Address Registrar](https://address.gov.mt/localities/) lists `San Pawl il-Baħar` in the Northern Region. This establishes administrative context but does not create a precise Qawra property boundary.
- The government's [Strategic Plan for Environment and Development policy material](https://pcrb.gov.mt/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/134-spd2-2022-060_28122023083132-002.pdf) describes the main urban area as including the Qawra peninsula and merging into Buġibġa and old St Paul's Bay. Treat this as a planning snapshot, not proof that two specific addresses share a walkable route.
- Primary HealthCare's [May 2026 Quality Service Charter](https://primaryhealthcare.gov.mt/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/May-2026-Quality-Service-Charter.pdf) includes Qawra and San Pawl il-Baħar/Buġibba in the Mosta Health Centre catchment. Services, clinic schedules and appointment rules remain mutable and should stay behind the official source.

## Everyday life and activity candidates

- [Welbee's store locator](https://welbees.mt/store-locator) identifies a Qawra supermarket at Triq il-Merluzz, Qawra SPB 1453. This is an operator-backed weekly-shopping example; hours, stock, promotions and the distance from a listing require a current check.
- The [Malta National Aquarium](https://aquarium.com.mt/) identifies its address at Triq it-Trunċiera, Qawra, San Pawl il-Baħar SPB 1500 and describes its Qawra Point aquarium complex. This supports a Qawra Point activity anchor without freezing prices, opening times or event details into evergreen copy.
- [Cheeky Monkey Qawra](https://cheekymonkeymalta.com/qawra/) identifies its Qawra Coast Road venue and publishes its own food offer. It can serve as an operator-backed dining example without reproducing rankings, prices or mutable opening hours.
- [Café del Mar Malta](https://cafedelmar.com.mt/contact/) gives its address as Triq it-Trunċiera, Qawra, San Pawl il-Baħar SPB 1500. If used, it should be described as a seasonal hospitality example and checked again immediately before publication.

These entries are examples, not rankings or a complete guide. Venues labelled only as St Paul's Bay should not be silently reassigned to Qawra.

## Map and mobility rules

- Use a fast click-to-load 2D OpenStreetMap overview that shows Qawra Point, the inner peninsula and the adjacent Buġibba context.
- Do not draw an inferred district boundary, sea shortcut, ferry line or straight-line walking route.
- Keep walking geometry, bus information, current road-traffic estimates and taxi-price observations as separate sourced layers.
- A property page may state a travel time only after a route request for that property's verified coordinates and the relevant mode.
