## Inside the walls

The local council uses L-Imdina and Città Notabile. Its account traces the fortified hill and the ditch separating the walled city from neighbouring Rabat. Use the Main Gate and the paved spaces around the palaces as anchors, then orient readers toward the cathedral square and the bastion side. Treat Mdina, Rabat and Mtarfa as distinct localities. The nearby Domvs Romana has its own entrance and museum information, and Heritage Malta places it outside the walled city. [Mdina Local Council](https://imdinalc.gov.mt/en/) · [Council palace guide](https://imdinalc.gov.mt/en/palaces/) · [Heritage Malta, Domvs Romana](https://heritagemalta.mt/explore/domvs-romana/) · [Rabat edition](https://www.2906.estate/link-marketplace/areas/rabat.html) · [L-Imtarfa edition](https://www.2906.estate/link-marketplace/areas/mtarfa.html)

## Visiting and getting around

- Heritage Malta places the National Museum of Natural History within Palazzo Vilhena, commissioned in 1724. Its access statement locates the museum on St Publius Square immediately inside the Main Gate. Link to the visitor page and access statement for hours and entrance details. [National Museum of Natural History](https://heritagemalta.mt/explore/national-museum-of-natural-history/) · [Museum access statement](https://heritagemalta.mt/app/uploads/2024/05/Access-statement-NMNH_2024.pdf)
- The Metropolitan Chapter publishes a visitor page for St Paul’s Cathedral and its museum, with access and planned closures. Give the cathedral square as an orientation point and direct readers to the current visitor information for opening times and tickets. [Cathedral visitor information](https://museum.metropolitanchapter.com/opening-hours-tickets/visitor-information/)
- Malta Public Transport’s destination guide lists services 50, 51, 52, 53 and 56 to Rabat. Heritage Malta’s access statement identifies a stop called Mdina for the Natural History Museum. Give the street or stop name to the journey planner for the trip and check current service alerts. Vehicle access inside Mdina is subject to the city’s special rules. Link to the regulations and council permit information for arrivals, deliveries and property visits. [Operator destination guide](https://www.publictransport.com.mt/goaroundmaltabybus/rabat/) · [Journey planner](https://www.publictransport.com.mt/journey-planner/) · [Vehicle access regulations](https://legislation.mt/eli/sl/65.27/eng) · [Council services](https://imdinalc.gov.mt/en/)
