## Why this locality is next

The current public inventory contains six listable homes with images under the label `San Gwann` and another three under `San Ġwann`. One further blended label, `San Gwann / Iklin`, cannot be counted as a confirmed San Ġwann home without checking its exact address. The nine strict-label homes make San Ġwann the strongest unserved edition candidate in the current inventory.

The public edition must normalize the display name to **San Ġwann** while preserving the inventory's source labels. It must not silently merge the blended Iklin record or neighbouring Kappara records into the locality count.

## Editorial boundary

- **Central San Ġwann** is the modern residential and commercial context around the parish church and main public spaces. A locality label does not prove a level walk, quiet room, parking space or short route from a particular property.
- **Ta' Ċieda and the archaeological context** may be discussed only at the broad locality level. The council records Roman remains and the Ta' Ċieda tower, but that does not place a specific listing beside them.
- **The valley edges** around Wied Għomor, Wied il-Ballut and Wied Għollieqa form a different physical context from the built centre. Exact terrain, access and route claims need verified coordinates and routed geometry.
- **Kappara remains a separate property context.** Primary HealthCare's own catchment list names San Gwann and Kappara separately, so a nearby Kappara listing must not be relabelled as central San Ġwann.
- **Iklin remains separate.** The blended inventory label `San Gwann / Iklin` requires address-level verification before it can appear in either locality edition.

The [San Ġwann Local Council geography and history page](https://sangwannlc.gov.mt/en/homepage/geography-and-history/) describes San Ġwann as a largely modern suburb while documenting earlier archaeological remains, the Ta' Ċieda Roman tower, Torri ta' Lanzun and the surrounding valleys. The council's [about page](https://sangwannlc.gov.mt/en/about-locality/) identifies the locality within Malta's Northern Region. Malta's [Office of the Address Registrar](https://address.gov.mt/locate-a-street-search/) remains the authority for the locality attached to an exact street.

Do not promise a fixed walk, commute, view, parking condition, silence or barrier-free access. Those depend on the exact building, entrance, terrain and current transport conditions.

## Facts safe for evergreen copy

- The council describes San Ġwann as mostly modern and records archaeological remains and cart ruts near Sant Andrija. This supports a layered-history introduction without presenting the whole locality as a historic village core.
- The council records Wied Għomor, Wied il-Ballut and Wied Għollieqa around the locality. These names support geographic orientation only; they do not define a property-search boundary or prove public access from a listing.
- The council identifies the Ta' Ċieda Roman tower and Torri ta' Lanzun among the locality's historic structures. Any more detailed architectural or access claim needs a dedicated heritage source.
- The council states that the present parish church was built during the 1950s, completed in December 1959 and became the parish church in 1965. Mutable service times and access arrangements must be checked separately.
- [Primary HealthCare's Gżira Health Centre page](https://primaryhealthcare.gov.mt/en/health-centers/gzira-health-center/) currently lists both San Gwann and Kappara among the areas it serves. Services and opening arrangements can change and should not be frozen into evergreen copy.

It is an operator-backed shopping example, not proof that it is the closest or best option for every listing. Hours, stock and services require a current check.

These are examples rather than rankings. Do not infer walkability from the locality name or freeze current offers, opening hours, menus and delivery areas into evergreen text.
