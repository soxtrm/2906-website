# Buġibba edition — sourced research draft

Language: English. Status: research only; no page, public image derivative or release asset has been published.

## Why this zone needs its own treatment

The public 2906 inventory endpoint returned three available, image-bearing records labelled exactly `Bugibba` at 12:07 UTC on 19 October 2026, from 529 records in the response. This is an editorial signal only. It is not a demand, popularity or value ranking and it does not describe the whole market.

Buġibba is a named zone within the San Pawl il-Baħar Local Council rather than a separate council. The council's geography page expressly lists Buġibba, Qawra, Xemxija and the other named zones within that council. The existing St Paul's Bay and Qawra editions therefore cannot simply be copied: a Buġibba edition must explain the shared council context while keeping Bay Square, the Buġibba waterfront and the neighbouring Qawra peninsula distinct.

Primary boundary sources:

- San Pawl il-Baħar Local Council, geography and history: https://sanpawlilbaharlc.gov.mt/en/homepage/geography-and-history/
- San Pawl il-Baħar Local Council, squares: https://sanpawlilbaharlc.gov.mt/en/squares/
- Planning Authority planning evidence, which describes the continuous St Paul's Bay/Buġibba/Qawra urban area: https://pcrb.gov.mt/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/134-spd2-2022-060_28122023083132-002.pdf

No source reviewed supplies a simple street-by-street estate-search boundary between Buġibba and Qawra. A later edition must never turn an approximate editorial frame into a municipal boundary. Exact address, frontage and verified endpoint come first.

## Editorial structure

Use three contexts:

1. **Bay Square and the inland street grid.** The council specifically lists Buġibba Bay Square at Triq Dawret il-Gżejjer. This is a civic orientation point, not evidence that every Buġibba listing is beside the square, pedestrianised or quiet.
2. **Bognor waterfront and harbour-facing edge.** Environmental Health places official point C25 at the beginning of Buġibba promenade and describes a rocky beach, Roman salt pans and a breakwater. This narrow evidence does not prove a harbour view, swimming suitability, mooring access or short walk from a property.
3. **Dawret il-Gżejjer and the Perched Beach edge.** The same profile identifies C26 at Bay Square and C27 at Perched Beach. The profile describes different shore surfaces and the historic artificial-sand intervention; it does not make the whole Buġibba coast sandy or establish today's access, sea state or crowding.

Qawra, the older St Paul's Bay core, Xemxija, Salina, Burmarrad and Wardija remain separate search contexts. The continuous built area and shared council services do not make those labels interchangeable.

## Everyday, food and activity evidence

### Health catchment

Primary HealthCare includes Bugibba within the Mosta Health Centre catchment. This establishes the catchment only. It does not place a clinic inside Buġibba or promise appointment availability, opening hours, travel time or the service appropriate to a particular patient.

Source: https://primaryhealthcare.gov.mt/en/health-centers/

### Civic orientation

The council's square listing establishes Buġibba Bay Square and its address. A later guide may use the square as a named orientation point, while treating live access, events, noise, seating, works and opening conditions as details to check.

Source: https://sanpawlilbaharlc.gov.mt/en/squares/

### Official bathing points

Environmental Health's BWP 19 covers C25 St Paul's Bay Waterfront (Bognor), C26 Bay Square and C27 Perched Beach. The profile page currently displays the latest 2025 classification, but a property guide must link to the live bathing programme for current notices and must not convert an annual classification into a daily condition claim.

Sources:

- https://environmentalhealth.gov.mt/en/ehs/wrau/bathing-water-profiles/bwp-19-st-pauls-bay/
- https://environmentalhealth.gov.mt/en/ehs/wrau/bathing-water-programme/

### Family activity

Parks in Malta records a Buġibba WaterPark season from 16 July to 30 September 2026 and age-banded play provision. This is useful dated evidence of a family facility, not proof that it is open now or that its schedule, access, age rules and sessions will recur unchanged. Any published edition must check the operator page on the day.

Source: https://parksinmalta.mt/park/bugibba-waterpark/

### Heritage

The Superintendence of Cultural Heritage's National Inventory entry 00056 records the Buġibba Temple in the Dolmen Hotel grounds, its prehistoric Temple Period context and Class B scheduling. It does not establish public access, opening, ticketing or a safe route through private hotel grounds.

Sources:

- https://schmalta.mt/national-inventory-and-scheduling-page/
- https://schmalta.mt/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/DC-00056.pdf

### Restaurant example

Peking Malta's own site describes its Buġibba restaurant as family-run since 2000 and locates it on Triq it-Turisti. It is one operator example, not a ranking or claim about the entire dining scene. Menus, prices, hours, booking, delivery and accessibility remain live checks.

Source: https://pekingmalta.com/

## Map and routing guardrails

No straight line may be presented as walking geometry. Walking, bus, current road traffic and taxi-price observations require separate sources, exact endpoints, mode and timestamp. No ferry is assumed for an ordinary Buġibba property journey, and no route may cut across the bay or sea. A ferry may appear only where a routing response includes ferry geometry and a current operator schedule is separately verified.
