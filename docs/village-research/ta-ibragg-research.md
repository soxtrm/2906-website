## Editorial boundary

Tal-Ibraġ needs its own practical edition rather than a renamed copy of the Swieqi overview. The Swieqi Local Council identifies Tal-Ibraġ as one of the areas within the Swieqi municipality. The national Address Registrar lists the official locality as Is-Swieqi in the Eastern Region; it does not list Tal-Ibraġ as a separate local council. Public copy should therefore describe Tal-Ibraġ as a neighbourhood/hamlet within the Swieqi municipality and retain Is-Swieqi for formal locality context.

Primary sources reviewed:

- Swieqi Local Council: https://swieqilc.gov.mt/en/ — municipality composition and historical context.
- Office of the Address Registrar: https://address.gov.mt/localities/ — official locality/council/region context.

Do not reuse the council's undated population count as a current statistic. Do not repeat its promotional social-class or quietness language as an objective quality claim.
