## Editorial focus

The Address Registrar identifies Santa Luċija as a separate locality in Malta's Southern Region. Its separate entry for Santa Luċija, Ta’ Kerċem is on Gozo and belongs to a different council. Use the Malta location and separate island keys in the marketplace, maps and public listings. [Office of the Address Registrar](https://address.gov.mt/localities/)

Frame the edition around two parts of everyday life. At Pjazza 7 ta’ Lulju, the parish of St Pius X gives the residential centre a point of reference. The council describes green space and planting as characteristic of the surrounding streets. The Garden of Serenity is a distinct destination along the green belt. Its managed landscape includes pavilions, a lake and paths. The local council lists Katerina Vitale Street for the garden. Confirm current opening at the site and follow posted circulation rules. [Council geography and history](https://santalucijalc.gov.mt/en/homepage/geography-history/) · [Archdiocese parish listing](https://parishes.church.mt/parish/santa-lucija/) · [Council garden listing](https://santalucijalc.gov.mt/en/open-spaces-gardens/) · [Parks in Malta](https://parksinmalta.mt/park/serenity-garden/)

Ġnien l-Iskultura is a separate neighbouring public space announced in May 2025. The government describes a red iron bridge and a koi pond. The same announcement identifies Ġnien Sembjożi as an earlier garden project. Keep the three names separate. A dated photograph of the Garden of Serenity cannot illustrate the 2025 sculpture garden. The council also lists Santa Luċija Football Club. A club presence does not establish public court access, schedules or booking rights. [Environment Ministry announcement](https://www.gov.mt/en/Government/DOI/Press%20Releases/Pages/2025/05/02/PR250723en.aspx) · [Council sports clubs](https://santalucijalc.gov.mt/en/sports-clubs/) · [Santa Lucia Football Club](https://santalucia.football/index.php/about-us/)
