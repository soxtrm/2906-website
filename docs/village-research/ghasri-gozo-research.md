## Square, lighthouse and valley

L-Għasri occupies northwestern Gozo. Start at the Corpus Christi parish church by Pjazza s-Salvatur, then identify Ta’ Ġurdan lighthouse and Wied il-Għasri as separate destinations. Visit Gozo records the church’s early twentieth-century construction and the independent parish established in 1921. Its lighthouse page identifies Ta’ Ġurdan as a hilltop landmark above the village. Check access arrangements before suggesting an interior visit. [Council history](https://ghasrilc.gov.mt/en/homepage/geography-and-history/) · [Visit Gozo, L-Għasri](https://visitgozo.com/places/l-ghasri/) · [Corpus Christi church](https://visitgozo.com/places/corpus-christi-church/) · [Ta’ Ġurdan lighthouse](https://visitgozo.com/places/ta-gurdan-lighthouse-il-fanal/)

The chapel of Our Lady of Patronage is on the village edge toward the valley. The official Salt Pans walking leaflet describes the gorge and the cliff-side steps to the pebble inlet. Use this as landscape context. Check current approach and sea conditions locally before suggesting an activity. A map pin at the village church supplies no route down to the coast. [Our Lady of Patronage](https://visitgozo.com/places/our-lady-of-patronage/) · [Visit Gozo Salt Pans leaflet](https://visitgozo.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/SaltPansWalk.pdf)
