## A high street and its side streets

The Address Registrar identifies Il-Ħamrun as its own locality and council in the Southern Region. Begin with Triq il-Kbira San Ġużepp, commonly called St Joseph High Road. The Local Councils’ Association identifies multiple activity centres, including the high road and Triq il-Kappillan Mifsud, alongside smaller hubs. Use these as editorial anchors rather than making a blanket claim about noise, walking conditions or access. The council describes the ground rising gently near Tas-Samra. San Gejtanu is a separate parish from the Immaculate Conception. The latter parish serves the area toward Gwardamanġa. The council lists the Madonna of Tas-Samra among its churches and chapels.
